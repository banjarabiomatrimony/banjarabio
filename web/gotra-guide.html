🚩 Gor Banjara Cultural Tradition

Gor Banjara Gotra & Lineage Guide

In Gor Banjara culture, Gotra and Pada lineage represent the spiritual and genetic heritage of our ancestors. Traditional matrimonial customs strictly prohibit marriage within the same Gotra or maternal Gotra (Mama Gotra exogamy).

BanjaraBio Matrimony has built automated Gotra validation rules directly into our matching engine, preventing accidental same-Gotra matches and helping families easily filter alliances while respecting ancestral rules.
