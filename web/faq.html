Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is BanjaraBio exclusive to the Gor Banjara community?
Yes! BanjaraBio is built specifically for the Gor Banjara community worldwide to preserve ancestral Gotra lineage, Pada matching traditions, and community trust.
2. How does Gotra validation work?
During registration on our app, candidates select their Surname and Gotra (Rathod, Pawar, Chauhan, Jadhav, Ade). Our algorithm automatically prevents matches within the same Gotra or maternal Gotra in accordance with community marriage rules. Read our Gotra Guide for more details.
3. How can I create and download a Bio-Data PDF?
Download the BanjaraBio app on Android, fill in your details once, and select from 10 printable PDF templates. You can instantly export and share your bio-data on WhatsApp with one tap. Learn more on our Bio-Data Maker page.
4. How does Photo Blur and Privacy work?
Candidates can enable Photo Blur in profile settings. When active, your photo remains blurred to public viewers until you explicitly accept a connection or unlock request. Contact numbers are also kept confidential until mutual interest is confirmed.
5. How do I get a Green Verified Trust Badge?
Complete Level 3 verification by uploading a front and back photo of your Aadhaar Card or Voter ID card in the app. Our admin team verifies the document within 24 hours to issue your Verified Badge.
