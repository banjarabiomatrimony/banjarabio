## 3. BANJARA CULTURAL RULES & GOTRA / SUB-CLAN SYSTEM

### 3.1. Fundamental Banjara Marriage Rule: Paternal Gotra Exogamy
In Gor Banjara culture, marriage between individuals belonging to the **same paternal Gotra** (e.g., Rathod with Rathod, Pawar with Pawar, Chauhan with Chauhan, Jadhav with Jadhav) is strictly prohibited as they are considered brothers and sisters (Bhaibandh).

### 3.2. Surnames and Major Sub-Gotras (Padas)
The app includes comprehensive sub-caste and Gotra mappings:

1. **Rathod (राठोड / 27+ Sub-Gotras):**
   - Karamtoth, Aaloth, Bhaanaavath, Bhilavath, Degaavath, Depaavath, Devsoth, Kodaavath, Kumaavath, Kholavath, Meghaavath, Meraajoth, Meraavath, Nenaavath, Paathloth / Dungaavath, Jhandavath, Kaanaavath, Khaatroth, Khethaavath, Khilaavath, Pithaavath, Raajavath, Raamavath, Raathla / Phulia, Ranasoth / Ranavath, Sangaavath, Sotki.
2. **Pawar (पवार / 12+ Sub-Gotras):**
   - Aamgoth, Aivath / Pammar, Baanni, Chaivoth / Pammar, Injraavath, Vankdoth, Inloth Pammar, Jharapla, Lunsavath / Nunsavath, Pamaadiyaa, Tarabaanni, Vislaavath.
3. **Chauhan (चौव्हाण / 6+ Sub-Gotras):**
   - Dumaavath / Chauradiya, Keluth, Lavidiya / Lavhadiya, Korra / Kurra / Mood, Paalthyaa, Sabavat.
4. **Jadhav (जाधव / 26+ Sub-Gotras):**
   - Ajmera, Baadaavath, Barmaavath, Bhagvaandas, Bharoth, Bodaa, Dhaaraavath, Dungaroth, Gangaavath, Goraam, Gugloth, Halaavath, Jaadhav, Jaloth, Jayt, Kagla, Kunsoth, Lokaavath, Lonaavath, Loolaavath, Maaloth, Mohandas, Pipaavath, Poosnamal, Salaavath, Sejaavath, Tejaavath, Tepaavath, Teraavath, Tuvar, Undaavath, VaderJhaad, Vadithya Jaajigiri.
