# Unique Selling Proposition (USP)

## 📌 Term Explanation: What is a USP?
A **Unique Selling Proposition (USP)** is the single, clear statement of what makes a product or service different from and superior to all its competitors. It answers the customer's fundamental question: *"Why should I use/buy your product instead of the alternative?"* A strong USP focuses on a specific pain point that competitors ignore.

## 1. Our Core USPs

### 1.1. Hyper-Local Gothra Matching (Cultural Depth)
* **The Problem**: In the Banjara community, marriages between the same Gothra (e.g., Rathod matching with Rathod) are forbidden. Mainstream matrimonial sites (like Shaadi.com or Jeevansathi) do not support deep Banjara sub-clan rules, leading to incompatible match results.
* **Our Solution**: BanjaraMatch filters matches based on strict community rules. The system blocks same-Gothra connections and prioritizes compatible clans, saving families from awkward social conversations.

### 1.2. Audio-First Profile Onboarding & Voice Greetings
* **The Problem**: Text-only profiles fail to convey the warmth, respect, and accent that are culturally vital when matchmaking in rural settings. Furthermore, typing on keyboards is a barrier for older, rural users.
* **Our Solution**: Users can record an introductory voice note. Parents can listen to the potential family's voice greeting in their native dialect (Lambadi, Marathi, Hindi). This bridges the literacy gap and creates a human connection.

### 1.3. Dual-Verification Trust Scores
* **The Problem**: Matrimony apps are plagued by fake profiles, causing fear and safety concerns.
* **Our Solution**: BanjaraMatch integrates digital verification (Phone, ID) with offline community referrals. If a local elder (community ambassador) vouches for a profile, the user gets a "Gold Trust Badge," making them instantly credible.

### 1.4. Zero-ANR, Low-Bandwidth Performance
* **The Problem**: Apps crash or lag on budget smartphones in rural areas with poor 3G/4G connectivity.
* **Our Solution**: Built-in dynamic caching (storing photos in persistent Documents directory to save user data) and the custom `GlobalWatchdog` thread manager guarantee zero ANRs. The app loads instantly, even on older Android devices under weak signals.
