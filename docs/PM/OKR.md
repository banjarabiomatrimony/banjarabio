# Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)

## 📌 Term Explanation: What is an OKR?
**Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)** is a popular goal-setting framework used to define measurable goals and track their outcomes. An **Objective** is a qualitative, inspiring description of what you want to achieve (the *Goal*). **Key Results** are a set of quantitative, specific metrics used to measure the achievement of that Objective (the *Evidence*).

# Case Study: BanjaraMatch Team OKRs – Q3 / Q4 2026

| Target Period | Q3 & Q4 2026 |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | Stability, Trust, Engagement, and Growth |
| **Alignment** | Product + Engineering + Growth Ops |

## 🎯 Objective 1: Establish BanjaraMatch as the Safest Matrimony Platform for the Community
*We want families to feel complete peace of mind, knowing every profile they talk to is verified and authentic.*

* **Key Result 1.1**: Increase the percentage of active profiles with a verified "Trust Badge" from **35% to 75%** via the Riverpod trust score migration.
* **Key Result 1.2**: Reduce profile reporting rates (spam, fake accounts, or wrong Gothras) by **50%**.
* **Key Result 1.3**: Validate 100% of reported profiles within 4 hours using the manual moderator dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objective 2: Build Habits & Drive Organic Engagement
*Maximize daily activity by rewarding users for active search and verification rather than forcing premature monetization.*

* **Key Result 2.1**: Boost the DAU/MAU ratio from **25% to 50%** utilizing the 7-day Login Rewards streak system.
* **Key Result 2.2**: Drive **30,000 new organic registrations** strictly through the in-app deep link Referral system (`banjarabio://`).
* **Key Result 2.3**: Increase the average number of messages sent per active user per day from **4 to 12**.

---

## 🎯 Objective 3: Achieve World-Class Performance and App Stability
*Maintain a buttery-smooth mobile experience, especially on mid-to-low-tier smartphones in areas with poor network coverage.*

* **Key Result 3.1**: Maintain Android ANR (Application Not Responding) rates at **0.00%** using the custom `GlobalWatchdog` monitor.
* **Key Result 3.2**: Reduce average image download footprint by **40%** by deploying persistent caching in the local documents directory.
* **Key Result 3.3**: Ensure that the Razorpay checkout conversion rate is **> 95%** with zero network-related payment drops.
