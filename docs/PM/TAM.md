# Total Addressable Market (TAM)

## 📌 Term Explanation: What is TAM?
**Total Addressable Market (TAM)** represents the absolute maximum potential revenue opportunity available to a product or service if it achieved **100% market share** of its target category globally, with no competition and infinite distribution channels. It helps businesses evaluate the viability of a market before investing capital.

---

# Case Study: Sizing the Banjara Matrimony Market (TAM)

| Metric | Sizing Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Community Population** | 12,000,000 (12 Million) in India |
| **Annual Matrimonial Spend / User** | ₹600 (~$7.20 USD) |
| **Calculated TAM** | **₹144 Crore (~$17.28 Million USD) per year** |

---

## 1. Demographic Breakdown (Target Universe)
The Banjara community (also known as Gor, Lambadi, or Sugali) is spread primarily across several Indian states:

* **Maharashtra**: ~4.5 Million
* **Karnataka**: ~3.0 Million
* **Telangana & Andhra Pradesh**: ~3.5 Million
* **Other States (Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Gujarat)**: ~1.0 Million
* **Total population**: **~12 Million individuals**.

---

## 2. TAM Revenue Sizing Methodology
To find the absolute maximum market size for a tailored matrimony platform like BanjaraMatch, we evaluate the demographics:

```
Total Banjara Population (12,000,000)
      │
      ▼
Marriageable Age Cohort (18-32 years old) = 20% of population
      │
      ▼
Target Cohort size = 2,400,000 individuals
```

If we assume a hypothetical scenario where **every single marriageable Banjara individual** uses a digital matchmaking app and spends an average of **₹600 ($7.20 USD)** during their search cycle (e.g., purchasing micro-transaction passes or premium packages):

$$\text{TAM} = 2,400,000 \text{ individuals} \times ₹600 = ₹144 \text{ Crore} \text{ (~$17.28 Million USD)}$$

---

## 3. Strategic Importance of Sizing TAM
* **Why it matters**: Understanding our TAM helps us realize that while the Banjara community is a niche market compared to general platforms (like Shaadi.com or Jeevansathi), it is large enough to sustain a highly profitable, self-funded, hyper-targeted mobile application.
* **Limitations**: TAM represents a theoretical maximum. In reality, a large percentage of this population does not own a smartphone or lives in areas without digital payment capabilities. To understand realistic expectations, we must look at the **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**.
