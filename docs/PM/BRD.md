# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 📌 Term Explanation: What is a BRD?
A **Business Requirements Document (BRD)** outlines the high-level business goals, objectives, and needs that a project must fulfill. Unlike a PRD (which focuses on *product features*), a BRD focuses on the *business value*, market problems, monetization strategies, stakeholders, and regulatory compliance.

## 1. Business Objective
Matrimony within the Banjara community (estimated at 10-15 million in India) has historically relied on offline, highly localized family networks. Existing mainstream matrimony apps fail because they lack:
1. **Granular cultural matching**: Searching by taluka, village, and sub-clans (Gothras) is vital.
2. **Affordable, trust-verified services**: Rural/semi-urban users hesitate to pay premium fees without guaranteed authenticity.

**BanjaraMatch’s goal** is to become the default match platform for the community, achieving 500,000 active profiles in 18 months, with a 5% paying conversion rate through localized, mobile-wallet-enabled subscriptions.

---

## 2. Market Problem & Opportunity
* **Problem**: 80% of matches fail due to lack of verification, leading to safety and trust concerns (imposters).
* **Opportunity**: By integrating a secure "Trust Score" and localized local-guru referrals, we create an exclusive, safe ecosystem. Mobile internet (4G/5G) is now ubiquitous, but payment and trust remain the final barriers.

---

## 3. Business Goals (KPIs & Metrics)
* **Monetization**: Generate $150K monthly recurring revenue (MRR) by Year 2.
* **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: Keep CAC under $1.50 through community word-of-mouth and localized referral schemes.
* **Lifetime Value (LTV)**: Target LTV of $18.00 per paying user (average matchmaking window is 6–9 months).

---

## 4. Monetization Strategy
Instead of expensive yearly plans, BanjaraMatch introduces **Micro-transaction Billing** and **Sub-30 Day Matrimony Passes** via UPI/Razorpay:

| Package Plan | Cost (INR) | Benefits | Target Segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Pass** | ₹49 (~$0.60) | 24-hr unlimited contacts & profile verification | Rural users / High-intent active searchers |
| **30-Day Premium** | ₹399 (~$4.80) | 100 direct messages, Trust Score Boost, 10 bookmarks | Standard urban/semi-urban parents |
| **VIP Matchmaker** | ₹1499 (~$18.00) | Personal relationship assistant, offline verification | High-income / Diaspora users |

---

## 5. Stakeholders & Constraints
* **Community Advisory Board**: Elders and community representatives must approve verification guidelines to maintain cultural sensitivity.
* **Regulatory/Compliance**: Adhere to Indian IT Act guidelines regarding matrimonial verification, storing data locally, and offering a robust reporting system for fraud detection.
* **Technical Constraint**: All payments must support deep links directly to Google Pay / PhonePe / Paytm without requiring credit cards or complex forms.
