# Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

## 📌 Term Explanation: What is SOM?
**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)** is the subset of the Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) that a company can realistically capture in the short-to-medium term (typically 3–5 years). It accounts for operational limits, marketing budgets, competitive pressure, and sales distribution efficiency, serving as a realistic near-term revenue target.

---

# Case Study: BanjaraMatch's 3-Year Capture Target (SOM)

| Metric | Target Estimate |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Connected SAM Cohort** | 1,250,000 |
| **3-Year Target Share (15%)**| 187,500 Active Users |
| **Calculated SOM** | **₹11.25 Crore (~$1.35 Million USD) per year** |

---

## 1. Establishing SOM Parameters
BanjaraMatch cannot capture 100% of the SAM immediately due to:
* **Awareness Lag**: Not all connected Banjara youth will discover the app in its first few years.
* **Competitor Alternatives**: Families using traditional offline matchmakers, community WhatsApp groups, or general platforms (e.g., Shaadi.com).
* **Marketing Budget Constraints**: Limited capital restricts launch velocity to state-by-state execution.

---

## 2. SOM Calculation and Market Share Target
We target capturing **15% of our SAM (1.25 Million)** over the next 3 years by focusing heavily on high-density regions (Maharashtra and Telangana) where our local brand ambassador network is established:

```
Serviceable Addressable Market (1,250,000 connected youth)
      │
      ▼
Target Market Share (15% capture rate) = 187,500 active users
      │
      ▼
Average Annual Premium Spend = ₹600 per user
```

$$\text{SOM} = 187,500 \text{ active users} \times ₹600 = ₹11.25 \text{ Crore} \text{ (~$1.35 Million USD) annually}$$

---

## 3. Operations & Strategy to Secure SOM

### 3.1. High-Performance App-Only Referral Loop
* Because families are highly connected, the custom deep-link referral system (`banjarabio://`) will serve as our primary growth hack. We expect this channel to acquire 40% of our SOM target at zero ad spend.

### 3.2. Trust & Safety Focus
* A major reason users choose specialized apps over WhatsApp matches is verification. Scaling the **Trust Score** system ensures that once a family joins, they retain and invite others, expanding our organic capture rate.
* Continuous ANR prevention (`GlobalWatchdog`) ensures our app remains stable, preventing churn and maintaining positive store reviews.
