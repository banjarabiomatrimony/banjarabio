# Privacy Policy - BanjaraBio

## 1. Information We Collect
*   **Personal Data:** Name, Phone Number, Date of Birth, Email.
*   **Profile Data:** Education, Photos, Family details, Gothra.
*   **Documents:** ID Proofs (for verification purposes only).

## 2. How We Use Your Information
*   To create and maintain your matrimonial profile.
*   To generate bio-data PDFs for sharing.
*   To provide matching suggestions based on filters.
*   To process payments via Razorpay.

## 3. Data Sharing
*   Your profile information is visible to other registered users of the app.
*   We do NOT sell your data to third-party marketing companies.
*   Data may be shared with law enforcement if required by law.

## 4. Data Security
We use industry-standard encryption (SSL) and secure backend services (Supabase) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

## 5. User Control
*   You can edit or delete your profile details at any time from the "My Profile" section.
*   You can control who sees your contact information via premium settings.

## 6. Cookies & Tracking
The app may use local storage to keep you logged in and improve performance.

## 7. Contact Us
For any privacy-related queries, please contact support@banjarabio.com.
