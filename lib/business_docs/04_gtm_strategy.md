# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy - BanjaraBio

## 1. Launch Plan
*   **Soft Launch:** Invite 500 community influencers for beta testing.
*   **Official Launch:** Coincide with a major Banjara festival (e.g., Teej or Sant Sevalal Maharaj Jayanti).
*   **Incentive:** Early bird 50% discount on the first 1,000 subscriptions.

## 2. Marketing Channels
*   **WhatsApp Marketing:** Direct outreach to community WhatsApp group admins.
*   **Facebook Groups:** Active participation in Banjara community groups.
*   **YouTube/Instagram:** Collaborate with Banjara content creators/artists.
*   **Offline:** Posters at community hostels and gathering centers.

## 3. Pricing Strategy
*   **Freemium Model:** 
    *   *Free:* View profiles, create one bio-data PDF, basic filters.
    *   *Premium (₹299/quarter):* View contact details, unlimited PDFs, advanced filters (height, education, location).
    *   *Trust Pack (₹99 one-time):* Document verification for "Trust Badge".

## 4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   Daily Active Users (DAU)
*   Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)
*   Subscription Conversion Rate
*   Bio-data PDF downloads/shares
