# Business Model Canvas - BanjaraBio

## 1. Key Partners
*   Community Leaders (Banjara Associations)
*   Payment Gateway Providers (Razorpay)
*   Cloud Infrastructure (Supabase/Postgres)
*   Local Event Organizers (Marriage Bureaus)

## 2. Key Activities
*   Platform Development (App Maintenance)
*   User Profile Verification
*   Marketing & Community Outreach
*   Customer Support

## 3. Value Propositions
*   **For Users:** Specialized community-focused matching, easy bio-data PDF generation, verified profiles for safety.
*   **For Parents:** High trust within the Banjara community, privacy controls.

## 4. Customer Relationships
*   Self-Service Platform
*   In-app Support
*   Community-driven Trust (Feedback & Reports)

## 5. Customer Segments
*   Single individuals from Banjara community (20-35 years)
*   Parents/Guardians looking for matches for their children
*   NRIs within the Banjara community

## 6. Key Resources
*   The Mobile App (Flutter)
*   Database (Supabase)
*   Community Brand/Trust

## 7. Channels
*   Google Play Store / Apple App Store
*   Social Media (Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp Groups)
*   Word of Mouth (Community gatherings)

## 8. Cost Structure
*   Development & Maintenance costs
*   Cloud Storage & API fees (Supabase)
*   Marketing & Advertising
*   Verification processing costs

## 9. Revenue Streams
*   **Premium Subscriptions:** Access to contact details, advanced filters.
*   **Bio-data PDF Themes:** One-time payment for premium PDF templates.
*   **Priority Listing:** Paid boost to show profile at the top.
*   **Verification Services:** Nominal fee for "Trust Score" badge.
