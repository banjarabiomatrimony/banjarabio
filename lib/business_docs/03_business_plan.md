# Business Plan - BanjaraBio

## 1. Financial Projections (Estimated Year 1)
*   **Target User Base:** 100,000 users.
*   **Conversion Rate (Free to Paid):** 3% (3,000 paid users).
*   **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):** ₹500/year.
*   **Projected Annual Revenue:** ₹15,00,000.

## 2. Budget Allocation
*   **Technology (Maintenance & New Features):** 40%
    *   Supabase credits, serverless functions, new UI components.
*   **Marketing & User Acquisition:** 35%
    *   Facebook ads targeted at Banjara interests, influencer marketing within the community.
*   **Operations & Verification:** 15%
    *   Manual verification checks, customer support staff.
*   **Contingency Fund:** 10%

## 3. Growth Strategy
*   **Stage 1 (Launch):** Focus on 3 major states (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana).
*   **Stage 2 (Expansion):** Partner with local Banjara youth organizations.
*   **Stage 3 (Scale):** Introduce AI-based matching and horoscope integration.
*   **Referral Program:** Incentivize users to invite siblings or relatives.

## 4. Exit Strategy (Long-term)
*   Potential acquisition by larger matrimonial entities (BharatMatrimony) looking for community-specific dominance.
*   Sustainable community-owned profitable enterprise.
