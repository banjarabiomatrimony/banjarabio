# Market Research Report - BanjaraBio

## 1. Market Size (TAM, SAM, SOM)

*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** 
    The total matrimonial market in India, which is part of a billion-dollar wedding industry. Millions of people look for marriage partners annually.
*   **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM):** 
    The Banjara community in India, estimated to be around 6-10 million people across states like Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
*   **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** 
    The tech-savvy youth and parents within the Banjara community who are looking for specialized platforms that understand their cultural nuances, estimated at 500,000 - 1,000,000 active users.

## 2. Competitor Analysis

*   **General Matrimonial Platforms:** Shaadi.com, BharatMatrimony, Jeevansathi.
    *   *Strengths:* Massive user base, advanced matching algorithms.
    *   *Weaknesses:* Diluted community focus, high cost, less trust in specific sub-castes.
*   **Niche Community Apps:** Community-specific apps (e.g., Lingayat Matrimony, Maratha Matrimony).
    *   *Strengths:* High trust, localized.
    *   *Weaknesses:* Often poor UI/UX, lack of modern features like PDF generation or verification scores.
*   **Traditional Methods:** Whatsapp groups, local brokers.
    *   *Strengths:* Free/low cost, personal touch.
    *   *Weaknesses:* Messy data, lack of privacy, limited reach.

## 3. Industry Trends

*   **Hyper-Localization:** Users are moving from massive generic platforms to niche communities for better cultural alignment.
*   **Verification & Trust:** Increased focus on "Verified Profiles" (Aadhar/Social) to combat fake profiles and scams.
*   **Mobile-First Experience:** 90% of matrimonial search happens on mobile devices.
*   **Value-Added Services:** Moving beyond just listing to providing PDF bio-data creators and horoscope matching.
